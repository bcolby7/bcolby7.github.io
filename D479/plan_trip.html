Taniti Island Sample Itineraries

Check out these multi-day itineraries to help you plan your trip to Taniti Island.

3-Day Getaway: "Breeze Through Paradise"

Perfect for: Weekend warriors and first-time visitors

Day 1 – Arrival & Coastal Chill

  • Arrive on Taniti Island; transfer to Coral Bay resort
  • Beachside massage and local smoothie
  • Dinner at The Driftwood Grill with live island music

Day 2 – Ocean Adventure

  • Snorkel the Kalea Reef Marine Reserve
  • Kayak mangrove tunnels or relax at Moonlight Beach
  • Fire dance show and luau buffet at Tiki Point Pavilion

Day 3 – Mini Island Safari & Departure

  • Hike to Echo Falls overlook
  • Lunch at Café Tamu and souvenir shopping
  • Evening departure

5-Day Escape: "Waves, Wonders, and Wilds"

Perfect for: Curious explorers and couples

Day 1 – Arrival & Local Flavor

  • Check-in and welcome drink
  • Visit Taniti Cultural Center
  • Dinner at Bayfire Bistro

Day 2 – North Coast Nature

  • ATV tour to Whispering Cliffs
  • Picnic at Crystal Cove and swim in tide pools

Day 3 – Sea & Spirits

  • Scuba dive the "Mariner’s Ghost" shipwreck
  • Rum distillery tour & tasting
  • Sunset catamaran cruise

Day 4 – Mountain & Market

  • Sunrise trek to Mount Aroa
  • Artisan market shopping
  • Dinner at Eka’s Table

Day 5 – Free Day & Farewell

  • Optional spa or paddleboard yoga
  • Brunch at The Nest and departure

7-Day Immersion: "Live the Island Life"

Perfect for: Families, digital detoxers, and world travelers

Day 1 – Arrival & Welcome Ceremony

  • Flower lei greeting
  • Settle into cliffside villa or eco-lodge
  • Cultural welcome feast with storytelling

Day 2 – Coastal Exploration

  • Boat ride to Sea Turtle Bay
  • Lunch at Waihouse
  • Evening bonfire and ukulele circle

Day 3 – Inland Discovery

  • Hike to petroglyphs in Valley of Echoes
  • Herb foraging workshop
  • Herbal soak at Tiva Springs Retreat

Day 4 – Community & Cuisine

  • Cooking class with Auntie Hina
  • Volunteer hour at school or reef cleanup
  • Dinner around a lantern-lit table

Day 5 – Ocean Day

  • Surf lessons or free surf
  • Dolphin spotting tour
  • Sunset at Lover’s Bluff with picnic

Day 6 – Choose Your Own Adventure

  • Options: horseback ride, hammock day, waterfall rappel, or art workshop
  • Night market food crawl

Day 7 – Farewell & Reflection

  • Sunrise meditation or paddleboard
  • Farewell breakfast at Palmshade Café
  • Departure
