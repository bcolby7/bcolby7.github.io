Getting to Taniti Island

There are several ways to get to Taniti Island. Let us help you plan your trip!
✈️ By Air

Taniti International Airport (TIA) welcomes flights from major cities across the region. Direct flights are available from:

  • Los Angeles (LAX)
  • Honolulu (HNL)
  • Tokyo (NRT)
  • Sydney (SYD)

Once you land, the airport is just a 15-minute drive from Port Taniti. Taxis, shuttles, and rental cars are readily available.

🛳️ By Sea

Taniti Island is a popular stop on many South Pacific cruise itineraries. The Port of Taniti is equipped to handle large cruise liners and welcomes thousands of visitors each season.

Popular cruise lines that include Taniti as a destination:

  • Oceanic Voyages
  • Pacific Sunliners
  • Island Escape Cruises

The cruise terminal is located directly next to downtown Port Taniti, making it easy to start your island adventure the moment you step ashore.
